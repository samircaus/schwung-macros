Knobs 1–8 on Move are macros 1–8. Each target moves one effect setting between From (knob at 0) and To (knob at 100%). Several macros can share a setting; their moves add up. A two-sided macro rests in the middle: Left targets move when you turn left, Right ones when you turn right. Drag a macro's knob slider to hear it on Move. Saving takes effect on Move within two seconds; your knob positions on Move are kept.
